The evidence base

The science of nature & the body in healing

The healing work is grounded in a living library of peer-reviewed research, on trauma, the nervous system, and the measurable effects of nature and water.

Nature

Nature heals, measurably

Across millions of participants, nature-based practices reduce anxiety and depression and calm the body. Even ten minutes helps.

Saeedy Robat et al. (2026); Bettmann et al. (2024)

Body

Trauma lives in the body

Up to 57% of chronic-pain patients have PTSD, versus 2–9% generally. The body keeps the score, and must be part of the healing.

Karimov-Zwienenberg et al. (2024)

Water

Water has its own power

"Blue space" is linked to better mental health beyond green space alone; cold water triggers a built-in calming reflex within seconds.

Britton et al. (2020)
